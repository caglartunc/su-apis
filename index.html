Center for Information, Communication and Language Processing

SU-CICLAP brings together AI and communication networks under one roof, advancing intelligent systems, hybrid LLM frameworks, and NLP research. We connect researchers and students to enable innovation, hands-on learning, and global collaboration.

Research

What we work on

Our work sits at the intersection of machine learning, language, and networked systems.

Natural Language Processing

Methods for understanding, generating, and evaluating language, including low-resource and multilingual settings.

Hybrid LLM Frameworks

Systems that combine large and small language models across edge and cloud for efficient, reliable AI.

Network Intelligence

Learning-driven design and optimization of wireless and communication networks.

Intelligent Systems

Distributed and trustworthy machine learning, from algorithms to deployed systems.
